Privacy Notice

Last updated 10 November 2026

This notice explains what information Sequence collects through this website, and how it is used.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

When you ask for early access, the form opens your own email app with a message addressed to hello@sequence.industries. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. When you do, we receive what any email contains: your email address, your name if your email app includes it, and anything you write. The same applies when you email us directly.

How we use it

We use it to reply to you, to arrange a pilot, and to keep a record of who has asked for access. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else receives data

The fonts on this website load from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page opens. The company that hosts this website keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the time of each request, to run and secure it. Our email provider stores the emails we receive.

How long we keep it

We keep your emails while we are in contact about access or a pilot, and delete them once they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Write to hello@sequence.industries. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
