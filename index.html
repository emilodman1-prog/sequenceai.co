Private intelligence for organizations.

Sequence builds a model of your company from your own data and runs it on private hardware to produce finished work.

The idea

Every company now has access to the same frontier models. What no model has is the company itself: its cases, its people, its commitments, its figures and its standards. That knowledge is the most valuable input a company has. Today it is handed piece by piece to consumer chat tools and re-explained by every employee, every time.

Sequence keeps that intelligence private and puts it to work. It builds a model of the company from the company’s own data, keeps it current, and runs it on hardware the company controls. The best frontier models do the reasoning, and the company’s own context makes the output right. The result is intelligence that belongs to the company: specialized to how it works, kept inside its walls, and producing work it can sign.

The product

Sequence connects to the data a company already has: documents, drives, email and internal systems. From that data it builds a model of the company, covering who works on what, the plans, the deadlines and commitments, the figures, and the standards the company holds itself to. The unit of work is the case: the people on it, the plan, the deadlines, the data and the figures.

On top of that model, Sequence produces finished work, not chat answers. It writes reports, packs, memos, notices and updates, on a schedule or when something falls due. Every claim is cited to the company’s own sources. Every draft is checked before a person sees it: citations resolve, numbers match the sources, nothing is missing.

People review, edit and sign. Sequence learns from every edit. A corrected figure becomes a correction, a rewritten section improves the template, and a style change becomes a house standard. The model of the company gets sharper every week.

Why it’s different

  • Private. The company’s data and its model live on hardware the company controls, not spread across chat tools.
  • Specialized to one company. It learns this company’s standards, structure and way of working, not an industry template.
  • Finished work. The output is something a person signs, not something they fix.
  • Cited and verified. Every claim traces back to the company’s own data, and nothing reaches a person unchecked.
  • The best models with the best context. Sequence runs leading frontier models and gives them the one thing they lack: the company. Customers don’t train their own models.
  • Efficient. Precise context means fewer tokens per piece of work and far fewer wrong answers.

Who it’s for

The corporate side of large companies: finance, operations, strategy, legal, HR and the CEO’s office. Anywhere people produce documents, reports and decisions from company data. Built for banks, private equity firms, the head offices of industrial groups, and retail and restaurant groups. Any company that wants AI working on its data without giving that data away.
